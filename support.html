Duotot Support • Contact & Troubleshooting

We are here to help you get the best experience out of Duotot. If you are experiencing issues or have feedback, review the common troubleshooting guides below or reach out to our team directly.

1. Common Troubleshooting Guides

How to pair devices over Local Wi-Fi

  1. Ensure both phones (e.g. Mom and Dad) are connected to the same home Wi-Fi network.
  2. On Phone A: Open Family & Sharing tab and tap Pair Device to view your 6-digit session PIN.
  3. On Phone B: Open Family & Sharing, select Join Pairing, and enter the 6-digit PIN.
  4. Once paired, care events sync peer-to-peer automatically whenever both devices are on your home Wi-Fi.

Voice Logging is not recognizing speech

  • Check that Microphone permission is enabled in Android Settings > Apps > Duotot > Permissions.
  • Ensure the Google Speech Recognition service is active on your device.
  • Speak clearly in short commands like "Emma had 120ml bottle" or "Wet diaper 10 minutes ago".

How to Backup and Restore

  • Go to Family & Sharing and scroll to Encrypted Backup & Restore.
  • Tap Export Backup and choose a secure passphrase. Save the .babycare file to your device or Google Drive.
  • To restore on a new phone, select Import Backup and enter your passphrase.

2. Contact Developer Support
